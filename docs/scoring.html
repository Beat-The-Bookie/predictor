How the scoring works

Every prediction is compared to the final league standings. The closer you are, the more points you earn.

Core idea

For each position in a league, you score points based on whether your predicted team is exactly right, one place off, or close to the correct finishing position. The top positions are worth more, and the bottom positions also carry stronger rewards where the league outcome is more decisive.

Typical points structure

Position Points awarded Example
Exact match 8, 6, 5 or 4 Predicting 1st when the team finishes 1st earns the highest reward.
One place off 3 or 4 Getting a team into 2nd instead of 1st still earns a solid return.
Relegation spots 5 Correctly identifying the bottom finishers is rewarded heavily.

How it applies on the site

  • The Premier League uses all 20 positions in the main leaderboard.
  • For the Championship and La Liga, only the top 6 predicted positions are counted in the main leaderboard.
  • Mini-leagues can be set up so that each host chooses how many positions count for their league.
  • Your total score is the sum of all your counted positions across the competitions.

The aim is simple: predict the final table as closely as possible and climb the leaderboard.
