The Story

The concept of predicting league standings is something that has been happening on a small scale for a number of years. We began in the 2016-17 season by noting down our predictions in our secondary school planners, and spending time at the end of the season manually counting the scores. After two years, in 2018-19, someone created a spreadsheet to store the predictions, as it was much more reliable than a piece of paper. From 2021-22, I took over the spreadsheet and automated it as much as possible. Following the start of the 2024-25 season, this public website was the clear next step. After a year of development, the website was launched in September 2025, and we are now into our second season!
